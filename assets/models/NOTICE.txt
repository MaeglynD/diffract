Stanford Bunny — Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory.
Original zipper reconstruction: 35,947 vertices and 69,451 triangles.
Source and terms: https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/

The mesh was centered, uniformly scaled to a height of 24 wavelengths, and
reflected along its depth axis for the optical coordinate system. A separate
area-weighted surface sample is used to calculate the complex hologram.
